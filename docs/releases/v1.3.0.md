# Social Safe Area 1.3.0

- **Show quick button** now appears below the introduction, before the settings,
  making the primary action accessible without scrolling through the controls.
- Settings are grouped into **Customize mask** and **Automatic opening**
  accordions, both initially closed. Opening either section closes the other;
  the primary action remains outside both sections.
- Home and About respect the system-bar insets, keeping their content padding
  below the status bar and clear of the navigation area.
- Refreshed buttons with a prominent brand-colour primary action, quieter
  secondary and text actions, rounded shapes, ripple feedback and disabled
  states. Accordion headers use a clear expanded state and simple chevrons;
  their contents are indented on both sides to show the hierarchy.
- **General vertical position** moves every selected guide together.
- **Subtitles vertical position** adds a saved offset from −720 to +720 dp to
  the subtitle box only, allowing placement towards the top of the frame.
  It remains relative to the general position: moving the general slider also
  moves the adjusted subtitle box.
- The subtitle slider and its own **Reset** are disabled when **Subtitles** is
  unchecked. Re-enabling the box restores its saved position.
- Subtitle Reset clears only the additional offset. General Reset restores the
  detected camera profile while preserving the subtitle offset.
- Existing installations keep their current alignment and subtitle placement.
- Updated labels and accessibility descriptions in all five interface languages.
- README covers now use a compact display size, with the English image in the
  English README; both guides include the new controls and current download links.

The APK is signed with the existing distribution key. It updates the previous
public release without removing saved settings. Android 8.0 or later is required.

Automated verification completed: debug and release builds; Android lint (no
errors); content padding below the status bar on Home and About; initially
collapsed accordions, mutually exclusive opening and accessible
expand/collapse actions; all eight mask combinations; subtitle slider input, disabled controls,
persistence and independent resets; rendered subtitle offsets in both directions,
top placement and addition to the general offset; physical-panel alignment at
0°, 90°, 180° and 270° with both offsets applied. The creator tested and approved
the updated interface before publication.

The corresponding GPL-3.0-only source is available in this tag and in
[the source tree](https://github.com/MarcorOnline/SocialSafeArea/tree/v1.3.0).
The release includes the signed APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
